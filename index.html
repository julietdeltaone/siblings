Welcome to Siblings. This app is a shared space for the four of us — Catherine, Ellie, JD, and James — to stay connected, share what we're into, and find time to actually hang out. Here's how everything works.
Getting In
PIN Screen
Enter the 4-digit PIN to access the app. The hint on screen will remind you what it is. After entering the correct PIN, you'll be asked to select your name — this tells the app who is adding or editing data.
Switching Users
Tap "Switch" in the top right corner at any time to log out and switch to a different sibling. Your data is saved automatically — switching won't delete anything.
Profiles Tab
Viewing a Profile
Tap any sibling's card to see everything they've logged — movies and shows they've watched, music they're listening to, places they want to visit or have visited, and personal notes or thoughts. It's a simple way to stay up to date on each other's lives without having to text.
Editing Your Own Entries
When you're logged in as yourself and viewing your own profile, you'll see edit and delete icons next to each entry. Tap the pencil to edit, or the trash can to delete. You can only edit your own entries — not other siblings' data.
Add Tab
Adding an Entry
Use this tab to log something to your profile. Select the category (movie, music, place, or note), type the details, and tap Save. It will immediately appear on your profile for everyone to see. Keep it casual — a show name, a song, a restaurant, a thought. Whatever you want to share.
Schedule Tab
Adding Your Availability
Type when you're free or busy in plain English — you don't need to fill out a form. For example: "Free the week of July 4th except Wednesday" or "Busy all of August" or "Available May 19th but only before 4pm." The app will interpret what you wrote and show you what it understood before you confirm. If it looks right, tap Save.
The Calendar
The calendar shows the current month and highlights days where multiple siblings are free at the same time. Yellow days mean 2 or 3 siblings are free. Green glowing days mean all 4 of us are free — those are the days to pay attention to. A small blue dot means someone noted a time restriction on that day (like "mornings only"). Use the arrows to navigate between months.
Overlaps This Month
Below the calendar, you'll see a list of all the days this month where 2 or more siblings have overlapping free time. If there are any time notes (like "before 4pm"), those are shown too. When all 4 siblings overlap, the app automatically sends a text to everyone to let them know.
Deleting Availability
Under "Your Schedule This Month," you can see your own entries for the current month and delete any that are no longer accurate using the trash icon.
Map Tab
Sibling Locations
The map shows where each of us lives, with approximate driving distances between homes. This is useful for planning meetups — you can see at a glance who's closest to whom and roughly how far apart everyone is.
Tips
Be specific with availability
The more detail you give, the more useful the calendar becomes. "Free the week of July 4th, but only afternoons Monday through Wednesday" gives everyone a clearer picture than just "free in July."
Keep profiles updated
The app works best when everyone adds things regularly — even small stuff. A movie you just watched, a song you're obsessed with, a restaurant you want to try. It's a low-effort way to stay in each other's lives.
SMS notifications
When the app detects that all four siblings have overlapping free time, it automatically sends a text to everyone's phone. You don't need to do anything — it happens in the background when availability is saved.
Google Calendar Sync

Select which calendars to pull into Siblings. Events will be imported as busy blocks for the next 90 days.
