Welcome to Siblings. This is our shared space — Catherine, Ellie, JD, and James. Share what you're into, update your schedule, and find time to actually get together. Everything saves instantly.
Getting In
PIN & Login
Enter the 4-digit PIN (hint shown on screen), then tap your name. You can only add or edit your own entries. Hit the switch icon in the top right to change users.
Profiles
Viewing a profile
Tap any sibling's card to see everything they've shared — movies, music, places, books, ideas, and upcoming availability. A green dot on the card means they've added something recently.
Editing your entries
On your own profile, each entry has a pencil (edit) and trash (delete) icon. You can only edit your own stuff.
Add Tab
Categories
Movie/Show, Music, Place/Restaurant, Thought/Note, Book, and Activity/Idea. For movies, music, and books you can also tap a quick rating. Keep it casual.
Schedule Tab
Natural language
Type when you're free or busy in plain English. Claude parses it and shows what it understood before you save. "Free the week of July 4th except Wednesday, mornings only Thursday" works great.
Importing a calendar file
Export your calendar as a .ics file and drag it into the import box (or tap to browse). Claude extracts all your events automatically.
iPhone: Calendar app → tap Calendars at the bottom → info icon next to a calendar → Share Link → save the .ics file → come back here and import it.
Mac: Calendar app → File → Export → Export as .ics
Undo / Redo
After importing or saving availability, you can undo and redo using the buttons in the import section. Undo removes the last batch of entries. Redo puts them back.
The calendar
Yellow days = 2-3 siblings free. Green glowing = all 4 free. Blue dot = someone noted a time restriction (like "mornings only"). Navigate between months with the arrows.
SMS alerts
When all 4 siblings have overlapping free time, everyone gets a text automatically. No action needed.
Map Tab
Locations
Shows where each of us lives with approximate distances between homes. Good for planning a central meetup spot.
