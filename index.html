Welcome to Siblings. This is our shared space — Catherine, Ellie, JD, and James. Share what you're into, mark when you're free, and find time to actually get together. Everything saves instantly and is visible to everyone.
Switching Users
Who you are
The app remembers who you are between visits. To switch to a different sibling, tap the Switch button in the top right corner of the header. You can only add or edit your own entries — you can always view everyone else's.
Profiles
Viewing a profile
Tap any sibling's card to see everything they've shared — movies, music, places, books, notes, and ideas. A green dot on the card means they've added something recently.
Editing your entries
On your own profile, tap the pencil icon to edit or the trash icon to delete. You can only change your own entries.
Likes & comments
Tap the heart on any entry to like it. Tap "comment" to leave a note. The person who posted it gets a notification.
Add Tab
What you can add
Movies & shows, music, places, thoughts, books, and activities. For movies, music, and books you can tap a quick rating (Nope / Meh / Good / Fire). The app cleans up and enriches what you type before saving.
Places
When you select Place/Restaurant, an address field appears with autocomplete. Adding an address puts a pin on the Map tab so everyone can see where it is.
Calendar Tab
Reading the calendar
Each day shows up to 4 colored dots — one per sibling. A solid dot means they're available. A dashed ring means tentative. Tap any day to see a full breakdown of who's free and when, shown as a day-view timeline.
Adding your availability
Tap the green + button to enter edit mode. A green dashed border appears around the screen. Tap a day, set your start and end time (or check All day), choose Available or Tentative, and tap Save. You can add multiple days before tapping the ✕ to exit.
Available vs Tentative
Available means you're definitely free — solid colored dot on the calendar. Tentative means you might be free but it's not confirmed — dashed ring dot. Use tentative when you're not sure yet.
Editing or deleting your availability
Tap a day where you've marked yourself free. Your entry shows at the bottom of the day popup with an Edit pill and a Delete pill. Edit re-opens the input pre-filled with your saved times. Delete removes it immediately.
Overlapping free time
When multiple siblings are free on the same day, the day popup shows an overlap banner. If all 4 of us overlap, everyone gets an automatic text message.
Map Tab
What's on the map
Each sibling's home is pinned in their color. Any places added via the Add tab appear as smaller dots. Tap a dot to see who added it and what it is.
Notifications
In-app
The bell icon in the nav bar shows a badge when someone likes or comments on your entries. Tap it to see what's new — reading the tab clears the badge.
SMS
When all 4 siblings have overlapping free time on the same day, everyone gets an automatic text. No setup needed — it just happens.
